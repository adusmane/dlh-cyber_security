1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) establishes the rules and requirements for the appropriate use of TechSecure Solutions Inc. information systems, networks, applications, and digital resources.

The purpose of this policy is to:

- Protect company assets, intellectual property, customer information, and confidential project data.
- Ensure secure and responsible use of corporate technology resources.
- Reduce risks related to unauthorized software, data leakage, unsafe browsing, and improper use of company systems.
- Define acceptable and unacceptable behaviors when accessing company resources.

This policy supports TechSecure Solutions Inc.'s commitment to cybersecurity, confidentiality, integrity, and availability of business information.

---

2. Scope

2.1 Applicability

This policy applies to:

- ☑ All employees
- ☑ Contractors and consultants
- ☑ Third-party vendors with authorized access
- ☑ Remote workers
- ☑ Interns and temporary staff

All users accessing company systems must comply with this policy regardless of location.

---

2.2 Systems/Assets Covered

This policy applies to:

- Cloud-based development environments
- Corporate email systems
- Virtual Private Network (VPN)
- Collaboration platforms
- Company laptops and workstations
- Corporate network infrastructure
- Approved mobile devices
- File storage and document management systems
- Software development tools and repositories

---

2.3 Exclusions

The following activities are excluded unless explicitly approved:

- Security testing activities without authorization
- Research environments not approved by IT Security
- Personal devices accessing corporate resources without BYOD approval
- Installation of unauthorized applications

---

3. Policy Statements

---

3.1 Internet Usage Policy

Employees must use company internet access primarily for business purposes and ensure that browsing activities do not introduce security risks.

Requirements:

- Internet access must be used responsibly and professionally.
- Limited personal browsing is allowed during non-working time if it does not:
  - Affect productivity.
  - Consume excessive network resources.
  - Create security risks.
  - Violate company policies.
- Users must not access:

  - Illegal websites.
  - Malicious websites.
  - Websites distributing malware.
  - Content containing harassment, discrimination, or inappropriate material.

- Employees must not attempt to bypass security controls such as:
  - Firewalls.
  - Web filtering.
  - Security monitoring systems.

- The company reserves the right to monitor internet usage for:
  - Security investigations.
  - Compliance requirements.
  - Protection of company resources.

---

3.2 Email Usage Policy

Corporate email is provided for business communication and must be used responsibly.

Requirements:

Employees must:

- Use professional language in all business communications.
- Protect confidential information sent by email.
- Verify recipients before sending sensitive information.
- Report suspicious emails, phishing attempts, or attachments.

Employees must not:

- Use corporate email for illegal activities.
- Send confidential information without appropriate protection.
- Register company email addresses on unnecessary personal websites.
- Send spam, chain emails, or unauthorized advertisements.

Email Retention:

- Business emails must be retained according to company retention requirements.
- Emails containing legal, financial, customer, or project information must be preserved according to applicable policies.
- Users must not permanently delete business-critical emails without authorization.
- IT Security may archive emails for compliance and investigation purposes.

---

3.3 Software Installation and Usage Policy

Only approved software may be installed and used on company systems.

Requirements:

Employees must:

- Install software only through approved company processes.
- Request approval from the IT Department before installing new applications.
- Use licensed software only.
- Keep approved software updated with security patches.

Employees must not:

- Install unauthorized applications.
- Download pirated software.
- Disable security protections.
- Install software from unknown sources.

The IT Security Team maintains an approved software list and may remove unauthorized applications.

---

3.4 Social Media Policy

Employees must protect company information when using social media platforms.

Requirements:

Employees must:

- Avoid sharing confidential company information.
- Obtain approval before publishing official company announcements.
- Clearly state when personal opinions do not represent the company.
- Follow professional conduct standards online.

Employees must not publish:

- Confidential project details.
- Customer information.
- Source code.
- Internal documents.
- Security incidents.
- Information about unreleased products.

Only authorized employees may represent TechSecure Solutions Inc. on official social media channels.

---

3.5 Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) Policy

Personal devices may access company resources only after approval and registration.

Requirements:

Employees using personal devices must:

- Register devices with the IT Department.
- Enable device encryption.
- Use strong passwords or biometric authentication.
- Enable automatic security updates.
- Install required security software.
- Use approved VPN access when connecting remotely.

Employees must not:

- Connect unauthorized devices to corporate networks.
- Store sensitive company data on unmanaged personal devices.
- Disable security controls required by the company.

The company may remotely remove corporate data from registered devices when required for security purposes.

---

3.6 Data Handling Policy

Employees are responsible for protecting company information throughout its lifecycle.

## Data Storage Requirements:

Employees must:

- Store business data only in approved company systems.
- Use approved cloud storage platforms.
- Protect confidential files with appropriate access controls.

Employees must not:

- Store company data on personal cloud accounts.
- Copy sensitive information to unauthorized devices.
- Share credentials or access tokens.

---

Data Sharing Requirements:

Employees must:

- Verify authorization before sharing information.
- Use secure communication methods for confidential data.
- Apply appropriate access permissions.

Sensitive information must not be shared with:

- Unauthorized employees.
- External parties without approval.
- Personal email accounts.

---

Data Disposal Requirements:

Employees must:

- Securely delete unnecessary confidential information.
- Follow company disposal procedures.
- Return company devices when employment ends.

Sensitive documents must be destroyed using approved methods.

5. Compliance

5.1 Monitoring

TechSecure Solutions Inc. may monitor:

- Network activity.
- Email usage.
- System access logs.
- Software installations.
- VPN connections.
- Security events.

Monitoring is performed to protect company assets and ensure compliance with security requirements.

---

5.2 Reporting

Employees must report:

- Suspected security incidents.
- Lost or stolen devices.
- Unauthorized access.
- Data leakage.
- Policy violations.

Reports should be submitted through:

- IT Security incident reporting system.
- Company security email channel.
- Direct communication with the Information Security Team.

---

5.3 Auditing

The IT Security Team will perform periodic audits to verify:

- Compliance with acceptable use requirements.
- Proper software installation.
- Approved device usage.
- Security control effectiveness.

Audit results may be reported to management.

---

6. Enforcement

6.1 Violations

Violations of this policy may result in:

- Verbal warning.
- Written warning.
- Removal of system access privileges.
- Mandatory security training.
- Suspension.
- Disciplinary action up to and including termination.
- Legal action where applicable.

Examples of violations include:

- Sharing confidential information publicly.
- Installing unauthorized software.
- Using company systems for illegal activities.
- Connecting unauthorized devices.
- Attempting to bypass security controls.

---

6.2 Reporting Violations

Suspected violations must be reported to:

Information Security Team  
Security Incident Reporting Portal

Employees may report concerns confidentially.

---

7. Exceptions

7.1 Exception Process

Exceptions to this policy require:

1. Written request submitted to the Information Security Manager.
2. Business justification.
3. Security risk assessment.
4. Identification of compensating controls.
5. Formal approval and documentation.

---

7.2 Exception Duration

Approved exceptions:

- Must have a defined expiration date.
- Must be reviewed annually.
- May be revoked if security risks increase.
