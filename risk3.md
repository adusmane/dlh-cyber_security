# Risk Evaluation: Cost-Benefit Analysis

## Scenario
SecureBank has a **Critical** risk with an ALE of **$300,000**.

**Proposed mitigation:**
- Implementation cost: $80,000
- Annual maintenance: $15,000
- Expected risk reduction: 75%

## Completed Analysis

| Metric | Calculation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Current ALE | Given | $300,000 |
| New ALE after mitigation | $300,000 × (1 − 0.75) | **$75,000** |
| Annual Savings | $300,000 − $75,000 | **$225,000** |
| 3-Year Total Cost | $80,000 + ($15,000 × 3) | **$125,000** |
| 3-Year Total Savings | $225,000 × 3 | **$675,000** |
| Net Benefit | $675,000 − $125,000 | **$550,000** |
| ROI | ($550,000 / $125,000) × 100 | **440%** |

## Decision

- [x] **Implement mitigation (cost-effective)**
- [ ] Reject mitigation (not cost-effective)

## Justification

Implement the mitigation. Over three years it costs $125,000 and avoids $675,000 in expected losses, a net benefit of $550,000 and an ROI of 440%. Every $1 spent returns $5.40 in avoided losses ($675,000 / $125,000).

The investment pays for itself quickly. Annual savings of $225,000 exceed the $80,000 implementation cost within the first year, and the recurring $15,000 maintenance is small next to the $225,000 in yearly savings.

The residual ALE of $75,000 is a much lower exposure than the current $300,000 Critical rating. The control would likely move the risk to a lower tier, though the exact rating depends on SecureBank's risk matrix. Since the risk is Critical and the cost-benefit case is this strong, mitigation is preferred over accepting, transferring, or avoiding the risk.

**Recommendation:** Approve and implement the proposed mitigation. Monitor its effectiveness annually to confirm the 75% reduction holds, and reassess the residual risk of $75,000 against SecureBank's risk appetite.

*Note: This analysis assumes the 75% reduction estimate is accurate. If the true reduction were lower, the case would still hold. Even at 40% reduction, annual savings of $120,000 would give a 3-year net benefit of $235,000.*
